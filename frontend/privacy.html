Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This notice explains how Euphorie, Inc., a Delaware corporation, handles information connected with its informational website and business inquiries.

What this notice covers

This notice covers euphorie.com and correspondence about the proposed manufacturer platform. The main website does not currently offer accounts, checkout, or a device service.

The task service at tasks.euphorie.com has a separate Task Privacy Policy and Task Terms. This notice does not replace or reduce commitments applicable to that service, including existing retention and deletion commitments.

Information involved

If you send an email, Euphorie receives your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your name, organization, role, contact details, and message contents. Sending an inquiry is voluntary. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal information.

Website requests also expose technical information to the hosting infrastructure, such as an IP address, request time, requested page, and browser information. Some of this information may be recorded in server logs for operation and security.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential business relationships, maintain correspondence, operate the website, investigate technical problems or abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these activities rely on legitimate interests in business communication and website security, steps requested before entering an agreement, or applicable legal obligations. Consent is requested where required for a particular activity.

Hosting, email, and fonts

The website is hosted using Amazon Web Services. Business email is handled through Gmail. These providers process information needed to deliver hosting and correspondence.

The website loads Instrument Serif and DM Sans through Google Fonts. Your browser requests font resources from Google, which receives technical information such as your IP address to deliver them. See Google Fonts privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

The main pages do not currently include advertising or analytics scripts. This does not mean that browsing creates no technical records or that external services receive no information.

Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers or other recipients where reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve a dispute, or address fraud or security incidents. Hosting and email providers may process information outside your country.

Retention

Retention depends on the purpose and sensitivity of the information, whether a conversation or business relationship remains active, operational and security needs, and applicable recordkeeping or dispute requirements. Technical records are also affected by hosting and provider settings. You may ask about retention of a particular message or request deletion using the contact below. Separate task service retention and deletion commitments remain in force.

Security concerns

If you believe a message or website request has exposed personal information inappropriately, contact Euphorie with enough detail to investigate, without including passwords or other sensitive material.

Questions and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. You may also have a right to complain to a relevant privacy authority.

Send questions or requests to euphorieinc@gmail.com. Euphorie may request information reasonably necessary to verify and respond to a request. Applicable legal requirements and exceptions govern the response.

Children

The website is intended for business contacts and is not directed at children. If a child has supplied personal information, contact Euphorie so the information can be reviewed and deletion considered as appropriate.

Changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Any additional notice required by applicable law will be provided. Separate notices and agreements will address future pilots or device services before they are introduced.
