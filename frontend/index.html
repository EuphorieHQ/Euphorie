A spirit of its own

The objects people love could have a voice, a distinct character, and a memory of their own.

Euphorie is building a software platform that helps consumer brands bring intelligence into physical products. Each object would carry a local model and memory, with an experience shaped around its purpose and the person using it.

A useful companion needs more than a voice model. It needs personality, chosen context, interactions suited to the object, and dependable service. Building that foundation for every new product takes time and specialist engineering.

Euphorie's goal is a shared foundation that brands can reuse across products. Manufacturers keep their identity and customer relationship, with a path to recurring revenue alongside hardware sales.

Local intelligence. Connected when it matters.

A model and memory on the object. The intended architecture lets the product carry its own intelligence, independent of a phone. Prototype testing will determine which voice functions run locally and which benefit from cloud processing.

Cloud support over time. Software and model updates, device management, maintenance, and selected connected capabilities would support the experience after the product is sold.

One foundation. A distinct identity for every brand. The planned runtime and development tools will let manufacturers configure characters, connect available sensors and controls, and shape interactions around each object.

Start with one useful object. The first planned prototype is a powered desk companion. It will test conversation quality, memory, response time, power, total cost, and repeat use after the novelty fades.

For manufacturers

A platform designed around your product and brand.

  • Keep your brand identity and customer relationship
  • Give each product a voice and a recognizable character
  • Remember context the owner chooses to share
  • Connect available sensors and controls to useful interactions
  • Reuse the core software across products with suitable hardware
  • Support devices through managed software and model updates
  • Launch through your existing retail and distribution channels
  • Explore recurring services alongside hardware sales

One reference product. A path to a brand pilot.

Euphorie is at concept stage. The first milestone is a powered desk companion prototype with embedded engineering support. Brand interviews and repeat use testing will shape the first feasibility project.

The proposed business combines paid integration, a software license per device, and an ongoing manufacturer service contract. Consumer brands with established gift or desk product distribution are invited to discuss a pilot.
