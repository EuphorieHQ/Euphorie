This page preserves the existing policy for task submissions and task orders. Website browsing and partner inquiries are covered by the website Privacy Policy and website Terms of Use.

What Euphorie does

Euphorie is a task execution service. You describe a task, pay $5, and we deliver the result to your email within 24 hours. No account required.

What we collect

  • Your email address (to deliver results)
  • The task description you submit
  • Payment information processed through Stripe (we never see or store your card number)

That is everything. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or fingerprinting.

How we use your data

  • Execute the task you submitted
  • Deliver the result to your email
  • Process your payment via Stripe
  • Respond to support inquiries

What we never do

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your task details with third parties beyond what is needed to execute them
  • Display advertisements
  • Build profiles about you
  • Send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in

Third party services

We use Stripe for payment processing. We may use model services (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini) to assist in executing your task. Only the task description is shared with these services, never your email or payment details.

Data retention

Task submissions and results are retained for 90 days to handle support requests, then permanently deleted. You can request immediate deletion at any time.

Data deletion

Email euphorieinc@gmail.com and we will delete all data associated with your email address within 48 hours.

Children

Euphorie is not directed at anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email euphorieinc@gmail.com
